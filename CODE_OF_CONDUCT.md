# Code of conduct

FrameBase is a small open-source project, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions
and reviews) to have a good time doing it.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and say what you'd change and why.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.

## Enforcement

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainers privately, through a
[private security report](https://github.com/agent9ether/FrameBase-Screen-Record/security/advisories/new) marked
*conduct* or a direct message to [@agent9ether](https://github.com/agent9ether). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
